## Problem

A client needed a weather-tight junction box in non-standard dimensions, on an urgent timeline.

## Solution

Using SolidWorks Sheet Metal, I partitioned the enclosure into fewer bend-formed pieces. More of the shape comes off the press brake and less from the welder, so weld length and fabrication time both dropped. I coordinated the bends with the press brake operator to match the shop's tooling.

## Outcome

Designed and fabricated on time, on the client's schedule.
