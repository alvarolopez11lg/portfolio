## Problem

A 3D printing client wanted storage for filament spools and tools, built from extrusion to match the look of their shop.

## Solution

I designed the case in SolidWorks, framed it in extrusion and mounted the side panels flush for a clean aesthetic.

## Outcome

Designs delivered quickly and approved by the client.
