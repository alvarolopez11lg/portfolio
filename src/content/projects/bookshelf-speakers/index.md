## Problem

I wanted balanced, detailed bookshelf speakers for less than comparable prebuilt options.

## Solution

A passive two-way system. I soldered a crossover circuit to send each frequency range to the right driver, and built MDF cabinets for their non-resonant properties.

## Outcome

Balanced, detailed sound under budget, and a working feel for how drivers and passive components behave together.
