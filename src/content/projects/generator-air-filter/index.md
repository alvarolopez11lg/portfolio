## Problem

A plant generator needed a retrofit air filter whose media could be swapped often, by someone working from a forklift.

## Solution

I modeled it in SolidWorks from on-site measurements, with hinged sheet metal frames that swing open, off-the-shelf hinges and latches, and weather-resistant, low-cost materials.

## Outcome

Swing it open, swap the media, latch it shut.
